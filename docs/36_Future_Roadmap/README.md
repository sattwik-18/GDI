---

## 2. Phase 1: Enterprise Core (0–18 Months)

- **Deliverables**: Complete implementation of all 37 specification documents.
- **Milestones**: FIPS 140-3 validation, FedRAMP High Authorization, SOC 2 Type II audit completion.

---

## 3. Phase 2: Population-Level Genome Network (18–36 Months)

- **Deliverables**: Enable cross-document population queries. When a document is uploaded, GDI automatically queries Qdrant across 100M+ historical genomes to detect recurring fraudulent document generators.

---

## 4. Phase 3: Cross-Organizational Federated Genome Network (36–60 Months)

- **Privacy-Preserving Fraud Sharing**: Financial institutions share anonymized fraud signals without revealing PII or underlying document content.
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP)**: Utilize zk-SNARKs to prove that a submitted document matches a known fraudulent genome signature without revealing document text.

---

## 5. Phase 4: Predictive Fraud Pattern Detection (60+ Months)

- **Generative AI Counter-Measures**: Train predictive models on emerging open-source and proprietary generative models (e.g., future diffusion backbones) to anticipate and flag synthesized document signatures before widespread misuse.

---

## 6. Advanced AI Research Agenda

1. **Multimodal Hyperspectral Imaging**: Integration of multi-spectrum hardware scanner streams (UV, IR, Luminescence) into the genome extraction pipeline.
2. **Quantum-Resistant Cryptography**: Transitioning HSM digital signatures from ECDSA P-384 to Post-Quantum Cryptographic algorithms (CRYSTALS-Dilithium / ML-DSA).
